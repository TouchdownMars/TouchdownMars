## Notes

- Card front text has no numbers and the reveal text has no numbers except mission names (Apollo 13, Artemis I/II); all numbers are in `Card_<Id>_Measure`.
- PMC page for George et al. 2024 (source `George2024`) showed a reCAPTCHA today, so it was not opened; the same article's full text was read from Europe PMC instead and cited as the new source `George2024EPMC`.
- `Veggie` and `Pepper2021` are existing sources and were re-opened today.
- DustStorm2018: the tau value appears under the June 10 update; "Sunday morning" is 10 June 2018.
- Apollo13Air EventDate uses the Flight Journal photo caption date; the OCHMO brief gives only the mission span (April 11-17, 1970).
